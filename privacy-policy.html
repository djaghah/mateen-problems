MatEN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

MatEN (“the app”) is a math practice game for children in grades 1–8 (about 6 to 15 years old), based on the Romanian school curriculum. It is made by an independent developer (Google Play developer name: Djaghah). We built the app so that it collects no personal data at all.

In short

  • No accounts, no names, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress stays on your device (and in your Android backup, if backup is turned on).
  • The app goes online only to download new exercises and their translations.

Data the app uses

  • Progress and settings — stars, gems, levels, trophies, your grade, your language and the sound setting — are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this progress in your Google backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.
  • The app never asks for your name, e-mail address, photos, contacts, location or any other personal information.

Internet

The app downloads updated exercise files and their translations from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr, with the files stored on GitHub). These requests contain no information about you. As with any internet connection, the server sees your device's IP address, which it uses only to deliver the file, under its own privacy policy. We never receive IP addresses or any logs. Without internet, the app works with the exercises already included.

Permissions

  • Internet — to download new exercises and translations.
  • Vibration — short vibrations as game feedback (only while sound is on in the app).

Reporting a mistake and e-mail

When you tap “Report a mistake”, the app opens your e-mail app with a pre-filled message: the language, the app version and, if you reported from an exercise, that exercise. Nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself. If you write to us, we use your e-mail address only to reply to you, and we delete it right after replying.

Children

The app is made for children. It collects no personal information from children (or from anyone), shows no ads, has no social features and offers nothing to buy inside the app. Gems are earned only by playing and cannot be bought. Parents can delete all data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Purchase

The app is paid once on Google Play. Payment is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

Translations

The versions in languages other than Romanian were translated from Romanian with the help of artificial intelligence. Mistakes are possible — please tell us with “Report a mistake”.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. The only services involved are Google Play (download, purchase and the optional Android backup) and the content delivery network that serves the exercise files (jsDelivr, files stored on GitHub).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: bogdansarac@gmail.com

O MatEN («a aplicação») é um jogo de exercícios de matemática para crianças do 1.º ao 8.º ano (dos 6 aos 15 anos, aproximadamente), com base no currículo escolar da Roménia. É feito por um programador independente (nome do programador no Google Play: Djaghah). Criámos a aplicação para que não recolha absolutamente nenhum dado pessoal.

Em resumo

  • Sem contas, sem nomes, sem anúncios, sem análise de utilização nem rastreio, sem chat e sem compras na aplicação.
  • O teu progresso fica no teu dispositivo (e na tua cópia de segurança do Android, se esta estiver ativada).
  • A aplicação só se liga à internet para descarregar exercícios novos e as respetivas traduções.

Dados que a aplicação utiliza

  • Progresso e definições — estrelas, cristais, níveis, troféus, o teu ano de escolaridade, o teu idioma e a definição do som — ficam guardados apenas no teu dispositivo, no armazenamento próprio da aplicação. Nunca são enviados para nós nem para mais ninguém.
  • Cópia de segurança do Android: se a cópia de segurança estiver ativada nas definições do teu dispositivo, o Android pode incluir este progresso na tua cópia de segurança da Google, para que possa ser restaurado num telemóvel novo. Nós nunca recebemos endereços IP nem quaisquer registos (logs). Sem internet, a aplicação funciona com os exercícios já incluídos.
